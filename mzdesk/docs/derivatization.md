# Derivatization

Reactive groups and the derivatized molecule for GC-MS, CIL LC-MS or LC-UV / FLD.

## Results

### Derivatives

- Full derivatization, one row per selected reagent that has a reactive group. Labels: reagent groups added.
- Mass is monoisotopic; heavy mass (CIL only) is with a ¹³C₂-labelled reagent. The ? on a row lists the groups labelled, EI key ions and caveats.

### Reactions

- All four CIL channels show complete substrate and product structures, including every label. Other reagents use functional-group schemes; ×n = number of groups.
- Choose a reagent above the scheme; the substrate highlights only its reactive groups.

# Quick Start

This page takes you from opening mzDesk to your first result in about three minutes, and names the parts of the screen.

## Run the example

1. Open [mzdesk.com](https://mzdesk.com).
2. In the sidebar, under **Workflows**, click **MS/MS Annotation**.
3. Click **Caffeine, library match**. The form fills with a caffeine MS/MS spectrum and its precursor m/z, 195.0877.
4. Click **Run**. The bar shows the current step, for example `Step 3 of 8 · Library search`.
5. Read the answer card at the top of the results.
6. Click **Session report** in the sidebar to download everything on screen. See [Session Report](/docs/session-report).

## Read the answer

For the example, the card reads **Caffeine · Level 2a · 1.00**.

- **Caffeine**: the top-ranked structure.
- **Level 2a**: a library spectrum matched and passed every check. See [confidence levels](/docs/reading-results#confidence-levels).
- **1.00**: the final score, from 0 to 1.

The line under the card says the spectrum cannot tell caffeine from isocaffeine. Click **Details** for the full statement.

Below the card:

- **Spectrum evidence**: your spectrum against the best library hit.
- **Ranked candidates**: every candidate with its level and score. Click a row for its formula, adduct and more.
- **Steps**: the 8 steps with their time. Click a step to see its tables.

## The screen

![The mzDesk window](fig:interface-tour)

1. **Logo and version**. The button next to them folds the sidebar to icons. Drag the sidebar's edge to resize it. On a phone it opens from the menu button.
2. **Module groups**. Click a group to open it.
3. **Workflows**.
4. **Internet search** switch, in the local version only. See [Privacy and Internet Search](/docs/privacy).
5. **Session report**. Downloads the results of every page that has one. Disabled until something has run.
6. **Docs**.
7. **Theme, language and the bug button** for [feedback](/docs/feedback).
8. **Page head**. The page name and one line: what you give, an arrow, what you get.
9. **Input form**. A `*` marks a required field. Workflows keep rarely used options under **Advanced**. See [Inputs and Formats](/docs/inputs).
10. **Example buttons**. **Load example** on a module, one button per example on a workflow.
11. **Run** and **Clear**. **Clear** empties the form and the results.
12. **Progress bar**, then the answer, tables and figures. A `?` opens a short explanation. See [Reading Results](/docs/reading-results).
13. **Steps timeline** (workflows). One row per step with its status, a note and its time. Click a step to see its tables.

The foot of every page names the tools it is **Based on** (see [Licenses and Based On](/docs/licenses)) and links to its **Docs**.

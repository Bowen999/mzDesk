# Platform

Which workflow can measure it: lipidomics, metabolomics, CIL LC-MS or GC-MS (decided mainly by extraction and derivatization).

## Results

### Platform

- Rule-based screen of which workflow should work, not a measurement.
- Yes: expected to work. Partial: only after derivatization. No: not expected. The ? on a tile gives its rule hits.

### Why and how

- Shown when CIL or GC-MS applies. Choosing a reagent updates the structure to highlight only its reactive groups.
- All four CIL channels show complete substrate and product structures, including every label; other reagents use functional-group schemes. Masses are monoisotopic.
